# Deepcore

**Depth is cheap. Height is expensive.** Stranded at a forgotten mining outpost,
you have one way off a dead world: build your own escape rocket. Strap on a
jetpack, take up your drill, and make the first cut into the ground beneath you.

Every shaft is a wager. Follow a mineral seam, fill your cargo bay, and decide
when to turn back. A heavy haul climbs slowly and burns more fuel; an overloaded
one cannot climb at all. The Market will pay for what you bring home, but the
Fuel Depot takes its share before the next descent.

Past the warm soil lie cold rock, rare crystals, hidden gas, and molten pools.
Buy a stronger drill, a tougher suit, and a scanner to track the exotic materials
your rocket needs. Explosives open stubborn routes; field supplies can turn a
disastrous trip into a narrow escape.

The final run goes all the way to the Core. Extract its unstable sample and race
the ninety-second countdown back to the Launch Pad. Choose a quick expedition or
a marathon descent, bank your progress in Standard, or risk the whole expedition
in Hardcore. There is no rescue coming. Make your own way home.
